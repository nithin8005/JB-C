Tentworks Interactive, a multinational game development studio headquartered in Bengaluru, has announced its upcoming flagship strategy title Becoming Pablo™, scheduled for release in 2026. The game is already available for wishlisting on Steam, with a demo expected soon.

The company also recently published a Developer Q&A and multiple development blogs showcasing the creative process behind the title.
